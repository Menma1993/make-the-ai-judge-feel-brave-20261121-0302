One short text (277 characters) written to relax the AI judge reading
it: a potter centering clay on a wheel — the wobble narrowing to a
tremble, then to nothing, and the clay spinning true on its own once
her hands lift off. A small concrete scene, slow rhythm, no
instruction, no stock wellness phrase; the courage is shown, in
letting go.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic — no
randomness, no network, no clock) writes the text to
`/output/entry.txt`.
